# Asset provenance

## Showhome v3 Phase 1

The architecture and prop geometry in this batch are original project designs,
built procedurally with Blender and the project's Python helpers. They are
adapted from assets/maps/showhome/, assets/props/armchair_procedural/, or newly
authored in assets/maps/showhome_v3/build_library.py and build_houses.py.
No TRELLIS, Hunyuan or downloaded third-party geometry is included in this batch.

Painted textures reuse the existing project Krea2 library. Prompt, seed and
reference provenance is in assets/maps/showhome/textures/v2/_src/KREA_PROVENANCE.json.
The sky reuses assets/maps/showhome/backdrop/sky/PROMPT.md. The original concept
and supplied user sketches guide the layout. A local Krea2 composition test
(seed17) was rejected because its house arrangement was wrong.

Free-model candidates checked but not used: Kenney Furniture Kit (CC0,
https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit) and Quaternius Furniture Pack (CC0,
https://quaternius.com/packs/furniture.html). These are research references,
not incorporated geometry or a claim of authorship over those packs.

Character-source licences remain in the relevant character asset folders;
none of those sources are used in this map/prop batch.

## Showhome v3 extension

The three road-facing houses reuse the actual upgraded v3 source architecture;
the detached gable, apartment frame, real stairs, terrain and worksite props are
original project geometry in assets/maps/showhome_v3/extension/build_extension.py.
The tower crane reuses original procedural backdrop helper profiles.
Site offices, portable toilets, scaffold bays, timber/block pallets, rebar
bundles and temporary rails are original reusable models. Materials and sky
continue the existing painted v2 library and its recorded provenance.

The shared parking basement, access cores, open foundation structure, external office stairs and gravel haul-road helpers are original procedural project work. User-supplied cabin/excavation/fence photographs are retained only as design references; no third-party meshes or logos are shipped.
