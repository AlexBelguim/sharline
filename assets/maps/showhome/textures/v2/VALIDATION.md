# v2 validation and look comparison

39 complete material sets, all four maps at 1024 x 1024. Every height PNG has an actual 16-bit IHDR and unsigned linear greyscale pixels.

Maximum absolute error in integrated linear albedo mean: 0.00006021. This includes groove cavity colour; clean and weathered members retain the family palette mean. The mixed-colour trim atlas is exempt from a single-colour mean: each strip is individually mean matched.

Normal vector unit-length maximum error after RGB8 quantization: 0.00640. Normals derive from physical height range and metres per repeat. Trim normals derive from the original kit profile functions with the sheet V physical width kept at two metres.

Wrap inspection: repeat seams are imperceptible in the painted colour. Recessed joints and roof course boundaries intentionally remain visible physical structure. The roof boundary was iterated until its wrap-edge comparison ceased showing a row change. The cracked asphalt tar curve and compacted gravel can have seam ratios above 1 because of their structured features; their feature functions are periodic.

The contact sheet and every wrap image place the albedo next to its concept surface crop. Every maps_compare image places albedo, normal, ORM and height next to the concept and painted cladding reference. The reference board covers the main close-view materials.

Comparison: sage and clay retain the calm concept hue, broad vertical strokes and soft angular patches. Thin grooves add cavities without putting a directional highlight in the albedo. Printed concrete remains cream with gentle bead shading. Roof remains charcoal with restrained half-bond variation. Ground colours follow the established palette; tyre tread, square repair patches, tar seals and joints add exact shape rather than photo grit.

Some requested surfaces (bark mulch, scaffold tube, loose gravel, corrugated site fence) are not shown at texture-inspection size in the concepts. Their previews use the closest visible earth, paving or site surface and preserve the established palette. These are original supplemental designs, not claimed literal reproductions of a concept crop.

Krea2 provenance: three reference-guided, mirror-quilted 1024² sources, seeds260081–260083. The identity adapter is the required repository --ref workflow; no T2I LoRAs. Precise structure and broad angular breakup are procedural. No new foliage cards or decals.

Physical 1 m / 5 m wall, roof, street and aerial comparisons come from build_houses.py. This data inspection does not certify the game height shader; the game session must use the manifest height ranges, normal convention and linear data imports.
